# Changelog

## [0.3.0] - Sprint 2

### Added
- Sign-in where the role decides the view, and shopper sign-up.
- Shopper view "Mi carrito": link the cart with its QR, live total (US05), budget (US04), simulated Yape / Plin / card payment (US08) and receipts.
- Pay by scanning the payment QR shown on the cart.
- Admin view: live fleet, alerts, cart detail and unlock with supervisor PIN.
- Spanish and English with a language switch.
